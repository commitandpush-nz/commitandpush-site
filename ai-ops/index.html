> HOW IT WORKS: THE CHRONICLE

A living record of your environment, kept in git and built as your team solves problems with Claude. Every server, service, vendor and decision gets its facts, the context behind it, a timeline of changes, and foresight: the risks an experienced engineer would watch for. Each entry carries a confidence level, from confirmed to hearsay.

Foresight entries drive reminders to Teams before certificates, licences and end-of-life dates land. A read-only copy can be published to Confluence, Notion or SharePoint for people without repo access.

> WHAT WE DELIVER

Every engagement is shaped by what you already have and where you're starting from. The work draws on five areas:

  • Context: the Chronicle, so AI understands the environment it's working in.
  • Infrastructure as code: changes go through git and pull requests, so every AI-assisted change is reviewable, versioned and reversible.
  • Access and guardrails: what Claude can read, what it can change, and what needs human approval.
  • Working AI workflows: incident triage, change prep, onboarding and documentation, running day to day.
  • Your team on board: people comfortable working this way, so it keeps going after we leave.
> SECURE BY DESIGN
  • Claude never holds a password. It works with the engineer's own scoped, short-lived access.
  • Read freely, change only through a pull request. A human approves every change before it's applied.
  • Secrets stay in a vault, never in prompts, repos or the Chronicle.
  • Everything leaves a trail: git history, pipeline runs and activity logs.
> HOW AN ENGAGEMENT RUNS

We work with your team, not at it. Your people know your environment; we bring the experience and a proven way of working. The Chronicle is built from their knowledge, so this only works as a joint effort.

  1. Assess: what you already have and where you're starting from. Every environment is different, so the plan is built around yours.
  2. Deliver: we build and roll out the change in your environment, alongside your team, starting where it pays off first.
  3. Hand over: your team running it, with optional ongoing support to keep the Chronicle accurate.

The Chronicle belongs to you and stays with you.
